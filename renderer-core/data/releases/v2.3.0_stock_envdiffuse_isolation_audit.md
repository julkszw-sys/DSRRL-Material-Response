# v2.3.0 — isolated temporary stock DSR EnvDiffuse cut

**Base**: pre-release source `232ac131fe55c670047bc633e6b13d3c8c95a306`, descendant of frozen V359-RC1 (`6e852cdc9085dd175159e564e223e0cc8589c535`).

**Mode**: compile-time `DSRRL_V230_STOCK_DSR_ENVDIFFUSE` on a **separate branch**. No release/working-base promotion. Keep one integrated .addon64 and exact source-to-build lineage.

**Target of this temporary rollback**: retain the *native DSR EnvDiffuse producer, t11/t13 texture and s11/s13 sampler, and stock consumer instructions* while allowing already-independent PTDE EnvSpec, SpecRGB, material, PointLight policy, other shader families and D3D11 transactions to operate as before.

## Critical non-equivalences and limitations

1. **Not a global lighting rollback.** EnvDiffuse returns to *stock source, resource, sampler and consumer* in the certified P_Metal HemEnv/HemEnvLerp paths. The resulting *final pixels* are **not** asserted to equal vanilla DSR: R3 postmerge visibility, R5 spatial normal, R11 common merge, material response and terminal/scene encoding still transform the composed result. Especially RX33/RX34 R3 removes a shared DSR postmerge visibility scalar, so strict end-to-end stock *pixel* EnvDiffuse cannot be claimed without splitting the common composition operator. Do not re-enable R3 scalar globally: that would regress independently working EnvSpec.
2. **No use of historical V13 diagnostics.** Both `DSRRL_PMETAL_V13_NATIVE_DSR_NO_TAIL_DIAG` and `DSRRL_PMETAL_V13_NATIVE_DSR_MATERIAL_MOD_DIAG` change unrelated SpecRGB and material/EnvSpec behavior; they are NOT enabled.
3. **No global toggle of HemEnv or entire resource bridge.** The P_Metal replacement shader can still be created; the EnvDiffuse-owned DXBC cut alone is bypassed, with exact source/consumer shapes retained.
4. **Non-P_Metal**: no new authorization on adjacent shaders or materials. Existing nonmetal MR/specular and stock DSR EnvDiffuse remain governed by their own previously approved receiver paths.
5. **No deletion of assets required**. Keep all EnvSpec/SpecRGB/normal/diffuse sidecars and manifests. This variant does not require EnvDiffuse PackedGI or LightBank donor; physically deleting the whole `DSRRL/EnvSpec/PackedGI` directory would also break EnvSpec.
6. **Reversibility**: original `232ac131` source and V359-RC1 binaries remain unchanged. The new mode is branch-local and uniquely identified by `[DSRRL V230 STOCK DSR ENVDIFFUSE]` and `DSRRL_V230_STOCK_DSR_ENVDIFFUSE`.
